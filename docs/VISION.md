#🌈 Project Vision

##🌈 One game, three missions

**Super Tux: Kerneltrix Wars** is planned around three mutually reinforcing missions:

1. Build an entertaining game with a recognizable identity and a cast inspired by the open-source penguin-game ecosystem.
2. Push **ImpossibleEngine** through a demanding, real-world production and publish evidence of what the engine can and cannot do.
3. Experiment with contributor-led governance while preserving the project's free-access commitment.

The ambition is a high-production-value, AAA-scale experience developed with substantial AI assistance. “AAA” describes the aspiration and production ambition; it is not a claim about current staffing, budget, content volume, or achieved quality.

##🌈 Creative premise

The starting image is a contrast between two versions of Tux:

- **Classic Tux:** continuity, familiarity, and the established open-source culture.
- **Cybernetic Tux:** AI, automation, augmentation, and new ways of creating software.

They should not be reduced to a simplistic good-versus-evil pairing. Their disagreement can explore trade-offs: speed versus understanding, automation versus agency, novelty versus compatibility, and individual creativity versus collective stewardship.

The broader cast may include characters associated with **SuperTux** and **SuperTuxKart**. Exact character selection, characterization, model use, and asset reuse are not finalized. The project must distinguish original fan-created material from upstream assets and must respect each asset's applicable license and attribution requirements.

##🌈 Naming

Current working title:

**Super Tux: Kerneltrix Wars**

Alternative title/concept:

**Tux: Kerneltrix Teamup**

“Kerneltrix” may become the name of the wider setting, a technology, an organization, or the conflict's central phenomenon. Its in-world meaning remains open.

The project is independent unless and until an upstream project explicitly agrees otherwise. Avoid wording or branding that implies official affiliation, endorsement, or sequel status.

##🌈 Relationship to earlier games

The project is intended to reach beyond a conventional small mascot platformer. The ambition is a major technical and creative step, with the scale and systemic depth associated with the user's “next level after PointBlank” framing.

This is an aspiration, not a claim that Kerneltrix Wars is a sequel to PointBlank or that it reuses PointBlank code. Any technical lineage must be documented separately.

##🌈 Player experience

The exact genre, campaign structure, multiplayer model, progression, platforms, and control scheme are still open design questions. The project should not lock these prematurely.

Early design work should answer:

- What does the player do minute to minute?
- What makes the two Tux interpretations mechanically distinct?
- How do characters from different source games fit into one coherent world?
- Which systems are essential to the first playable vertical slice?
- What can be demonstrated as a meaningful ImpossibleEngine benchmark?

##🌈 Guiding principles

- The game must remain enjoyable without requiring token ownership.
- Governance must not become a pay-to-win or gameplay power system.
- The free edition is a permanent project commitment.
- Contributions should be evaluated transparently.
- AI-generated code and assets must receive the same review and provenance scrutiny as other contributions.
- Upstream communities and creators should be treated as collaborators and rights-holders, not as an asset quarry.
- Performance and capability claims must be reproducible.
